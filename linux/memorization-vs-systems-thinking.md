When I started learning Linux and cybersecurity, I assumed experts had almost every command memorized. Finding out that even experienced professionals regularly use man pages, help commands, and documentation was surprising at first.



Over time, I realized that the real skill is not memorizing every flag or command syntax. The deeper skill is systems thinking: understanding how things connect, thinking from first principles, and being creative enough to solve problems in unfamiliar situations. This greatly changed how I approach learning and practising cybersecurity.
